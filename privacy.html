Privacy policy

Home Assistant Jarvis is used only by its owner, on a Home Assistant server in the owner's home.

  • With the owner's permission it reads and creates events in the owner's Google Calendar.
  • Calendar data stays on the owner's home server. Parts of it may be sent to the language model provider configured by the owner only to answer the owner's own questions.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties for any other purpose, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
